OutputCalc

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

OutputCalc is a free Android application developed by StanleyUA. It helps users track work productivity, production batches, daily norms, and estimated salary. The application is designed to operate locally without requiring an account or an internet connection.

1. Information You Enter

OutputCalc allows you to enter and manage information such as work dates, production quantities, daily norms, production batches, salary rates, and application settings.

This information is stored locally on your device. StanleyUA does not operate a server that receives or stores this information.

2. Data Collection and Sharing

OutputCalc does not collect or transmit your work records, salary information, or other application data to StanleyUA.

The application does not include:

  • User registration or online accounts
  • Advertising services
  • Analytics or user-tracking services
  • Third-party telemetry or crash-reporting services

StanleyUA does not sell your application data or share it with advertisers.

3. Local Data Storage

Your application records and preferences are stored on your Android device using local storage. You can manage these records within the application.

Clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the application may remove locally stored data, subject to any backups maintained by you or your device's backup system.

4. Manual Backup and Restore

OutputCalc provides a manual backup and restore feature. When exporting a backup, you choose where to save the file using the Android system file picker.

The backup may contain your work records, production batches, salary settings, and related application data.

You are responsible for choosing a secure storage location and managing any copies you create. If you select a cloud storage provider or another third-party destination, its own privacy practices may apply.

Restoring a backup is initiated by you through the application's import functionality.

5. Android System Backup

OutputCalc permits Android system backup functionality. Depending on your device, Android version, backup settings, and applicable backup rules, some application data may be included in backups managed by Android or your device's backup provider.

Such backups are managed by the operating system or backup provider, not by StanleyUA. Their availability, storage, restoration, and retention are subject to the relevant provider's policies and settings.

6. Data Security

OutputCalc does not require an internet connection to perform its core functions. Application data is maintained in Android's local application storage.

The security of exported backup files depends on where and how you choose to store them. We recommend keeping backups in a secure location.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored data remains on your device until you modify or remove it, clear the application's storage, or uninstall the application, subject to Android's backup and restore behavior.

Manually exported backup files are not automatically deleted when the application is uninstalled. You can delete those files from their storage locations separately.

StanleyUA cannot remotely access or delete your locally stored application records because they are not stored on a StanleyUA server.

8. Children's Privacy

OutputCalc is intended as a general productivity and work-record application. It is not specifically designed for children and does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's features or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
